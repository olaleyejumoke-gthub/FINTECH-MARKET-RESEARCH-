The Nigerian fintech landscape, while vibrant, still presents major friction points for the everyday consumer. Users are often forced to juggle multiple apps or platforms to handle their full financial life: one for local transfers and bill payments, another for managing a USD account or virtual card for international commerce, and a third for gift card purchases. This fragmented, inconvenient, and often opaque system erodes user trust and creates a significant barrier to financial fluidity. Furthermore, the complexity and high friction of obtaining fully verified accounts and the lack of transparent pricing are major consumer pain points that our competitors have not fully resolved. Here is a detailed market research that includes reviewing product offerings, pricing structures, and user journeys of selected competitors. Analyzing transaction fees, KYC requirements, foreign exchange markups, card charges, and bill payment services. The goal is to understand the cost implications for users and how transparency in fees can build trust.
